Welcome to the **IT & Systems Infrastructure** section of Sturdy Base. This section covers foundational infrastructure concepts, networking, enterprise systems, and operational practices.

## Core Focus Areas

- **Networking & Connectivity:** IP routing, DNS, subnets, firewalls, and VPNs.
- **Operating Systems & Linux:** Linux server hardening, systemd, kernel tuning, and automation.
- **Identity & Access Management:** Active Directory, LDAP, OAuth2/OIDC, and zero-trust principles.
- **Storage & Backup:** Block, file, and object storage, RAID configurations, and disaster recovery strategies.
- **Observability:** Metrics, logging, alerting, and telemetry infrastructure.
